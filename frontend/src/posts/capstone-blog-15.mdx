For this week, I continued developing the Piggy app, this time further enhancing the design. I had already figured out the colors, but my main issue was that I was not satisfied with the font I had chosen, Bricolage Grotesk. Although I liked my initial choice, it was a bit difficult to read at times, especially with numbers, which were compressed and looked too similar to one another. So, I visited Google Fonts and started searching for a new alternative. I ended up with ten options, which I then narrowed down to five. I found it hard to select just one, so I asked each of my classmates and a colleague to choose their favorite from the five. Ultimately, the Onest font won, which I really liked because the rounded letters matched well with the rounded corners in my design.

I then started redesigning the home page, for greater clarity and better separation of content between the monthly report and the spending tracker. I also changed the way the legend of the pie chart appears by creating text bubbles that connect to each segment with its corresponding label. The last major feature that is missing is the “rewards” screen, which I envision having a streak to encourage people to use the app daily, and badges that are earned through using the app. To begin working on it, I came up with these badges:

| Title              | Description                                              |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Baby’s First Steps | First transaction                                        |
| Under Control      | Didn’t overspend for a month                             |
| Cheap Life         | Saved more than 50% of your monthly income               |
| Shopaholic         | Spent more than 50% of your monthly earnings on wants    |
| Like I Like It     | Create your first custom category                        |
| Steady Planner     | Stay within budget for three months consecutively        |
| Expert Planner     | Stay within budget for the entire year                   |
| A Little Treat     | Have restaurants as your top category for one month      |
| Smart Spender      | Spent more on needs than wants in a month                |
| Perfectionist      | Spent exactly what was budgeted in a month               |
| Minimalist         | Zero expenses in the shopping category for a whole month |

Next, I jumped onto Figma to create the icons of the badges. Each has its own emoji and color palette, with its title displayed in a banner across the middle. When a badge hasn’t been earned, it appears with a grey background and a lock emoji. I created this first draft of how the design of this screen will look:
