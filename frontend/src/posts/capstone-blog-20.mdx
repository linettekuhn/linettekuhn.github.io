This was my final week of capstone and therefore the final week of my time here at Full Sail University. During this week, I put my Piggy app development on pause while I finish up my classes and assignments and prepare for graduation. I worked alongside my teammates to prepare our final presentation and walkthrough video of the work we did on Echelon these past few months. Through this, we reviewed the highs and lows of the process as well as our individual contributions to the project. We also got to see all of our work put together, as for the walkthrough video we combined all the components we’ve made into a screen. Down below you can see what this looks like.

![Echelon Reflect Mirror Demo](/gifs/echelon_demo.gif)
_Echelon Reflect Mirror Demo_

Graduation is a very big deal for me, so the rest of my time this week was spent preparing for this event and greeting my friends and family who came to visit. The ceremony itself was such a lively and fun event. I got to see so many people I’ve met during my time here walk the finish line. And then, I got to do it myself, which was pretty cool!

So, what’s next? This week I also had a chance to have a conversation with Career Development. I’ve contacted them a few times before, but this meeting focused on creating my career strategy plan. We discussed my goals and expectations for the future, and it made me a little more confident knowing all the resources I have at my disposal through the Career Development Team. All that’s left to do now is find a job and live out my software engineering dreams.
