While I was looking for things to work on this week, I remembered how a few months back my sister had a problem where she couldn’t find a budgeting app that fit all her needs, and she wanted me to help her out by creating one for her. At the time, I was too busy and doubted my skills, so I declined, but now I feel prepared enough to tackle this task. Even though I have experience with Android development using Kotlin and Compose, I wanted the app to be multiplatform, and especially include iOS devices (since that’s what my sister has). I figured the easiest transition for my frontend skills would be through React Native, since it works similarly to React.js, which I’m very familiar with. I could develop for both Android and iOS devices at the same time while using components and state-driven development, which is exactly what I needed. So I began right away, working on the design for a proof-of-concept app in Figma. I came up with these wireframes for the time being, but I plan on adding a lot more dimension and consistency to the design as I develop the app’s UI.

![Budgeting app initial design](/images/budget-app-design.png)
_Budgeting app initial design_

I also began getting familiar with the structure and differences that come with React Native and learned about a framework called Expo, which would facilitate my development by providing a way to build iOS apps without the need for a Mac or Xcode, and give me a template app to begin with. From there, I created the navigation bar at the bottom, which is called a tab bar in mobile development, and the UI for the page that adds transactions.

![Add new transaction screen in budgeting app](/images/budget-app-transaction-page.jpg)
